01 Profile

Krishna Sagar is Managing Partner at BlackMatter Builders & Developers, BlackMatter Hospitality, and TATWA. He leads strategic vision and operational execution across residential, commercial, retail, and hospitality ventures, including Fuchsia Gardens in Irinjalakuda and WAAW Mall.

He is Chief Executive Officer of Car Club Exotic Machines, where he has served since 2017, and Managing Director at CARVO Ventures. A technologist by training, he previously served as CTO at Krysllio Technologies (2017–2024) and General Manager at Smart Infotech (2016–2017).
